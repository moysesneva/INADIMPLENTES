1. Title

Inspect and Validate the Spreadsheet Structure for a CNA Debt Negotiation System

2. Context

I am building a local-first debt negotiation and delinquency management system for CNA schools.

The attached spreadsheet currently refers to CNA VIVENDAS and covers years 2015 through 2025. This spreadsheet will be the primary source for the first version of the system.

Before generating any Django models, database schema, business logic, or UI, the first task is to inspect the spreadsheet structure and validate the real column mapping.

This system must later support:
- CNA VIVENDAS
- CNA ITANHANGÁ

A critical business rule already defined:
- debtor consolidation must be based on "Categoria do Financeiro"
- all periods for the same debtor/category must be consolidated together
- original row-level detail must be preserved

The system will later support three collection scenarios:
1. COBRANÇA DIRETA CNA
2. COBRANÇA SERASA
3. COBRANÇA FEIRÃO SERASA

The scenario mappings must only be accepted after spreadsheet validation.
